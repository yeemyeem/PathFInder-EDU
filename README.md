# PathFinder EDU – Interactive Career Pathfinder Web App

## 📌 Project Overview
PathFinder EDU is an interactive, web-based career pathfinder application developed by senior high school students as part of an academic research project. The application is designed to assist senior high school students in exploring potential career paths based on their interests and personality traits using the RIASEC (Realistic, Investigative, Artistic, Social, Enterprising, Conventional) model.

This web application serves as the intervention tool used in the research study entitled:

**“Effect of Using an Interactive Career Pathfinder Web App on the Career Decision-Making Self-Efficacy of Senior High School Students at the Imus Institute of Science and Technology: A Quasi-Experimental Study.”**

---

## 🎯 Purpose of the Study
The primary objective of this project is to examine the effect of an interactive, RIASEC-based career guidance web application on the career decision-making self-efficacy of senior high school students.

Specifically, the application aims to:
- Help students identify their dominant career personality types
- Provide career suggestions aligned with academic strands (STEM, ABM, HUMSS)
- Support students in reflecting on their career interests
- Serve as a digital intervention for a quasi-experimental research design

---

## 🧠 Key Features
- 30-item interactive RIASEC questionnaire
- Yes/No response format for ease of use
- Automatic calculation of top three RIASEC personality types
- Career suggestions mapped to senior high school academic strands
- Retro game-inspired interface
- Background music and button sound effects
- Mobile-friendly and browser-based
- Screenshot-ready results page for research documentation

---

## 🧪 Research Design Context
This application was utilized as part of a **quasi-experimental research design**, where participants interacted with the web app as the treatment/intervention. Data gathered from the study were used to analyze changes in students’ career decision-making self-efficacy.

The application is intended strictly for **educational and research purposes only**.
