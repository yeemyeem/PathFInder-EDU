Interactive Career Pathfinder

Developed as part of a research study at the Imus Institute of Science and Technology

Welcome!

Interactive Career Pathfinder is a web-based career guidance tool designed to help senior high school students discover career paths that match their interests and personality using the RIASEC model.

About the Web App

This application presents a 30-item interactive questionnaire. Based on your responses, the system identifies your top three career personality types and suggests possible careers aligned with different academic strands.

Instructions

When you are ready, click “Start Quiz.” Take your time and answer each question honestly and from your heart. There are no right or wrong answers—this quiz is meant to help you understand yourself better.

If you change your mind, you can always go back or retake the quiz.

Once you receive your results, don’t forget to take a screenshot of the results page as evidence of completion.

About This Project

This web application was developed by students as a required academic research project of the Imus Institute of Science and Technology.

It aims to help students explore possible career paths using a RIASEC-based assessment in an interactive and user-friendly way.
