Welcome!

PathFinder EDU is an interactive, web-based career guidance application designed to help senior high school students explore career paths that align with their interests and personality. This tool uses the RIASEC career model to provide meaningful insights and guidance for future academic and career decisions.

About the Web App

This application features a 30-item interactive questionnaire. Based on your responses, the system identifies your top three career personality types and suggests possible careers aligned with the STEM, ABM, and HUMSS academic strands.

Instructions

  1. Click Start Quiz when you are ready.
  2. Answer each question honestly—there are no right or wrong answers.
  3. Take your time and reflect on each question.
  4. You may go back or retake the quiz at any time.
  5. After completing the quiz, review your results and take a screenshot as evidence of completion.

RIASEC Career Model

The RIASEC model is a career guidance framework developed by John Holland. It explains how people’s interests and personalities relate to different career paths.

This model groups interests into six personality types:

R – Realistic: Hands-on, practical, and technical activities

I – Investigative: Analytical, scientific, and problem-solving tasks

A – Artistic: Creative, expressive, and imaginative activities

S – Social: Helping, teaching, and supporting others

E – Enterprising: Leading, persuading, and decision-making

C – Conventional: Organizing, managing data, and structured work

PathFinder EDU uses the RIASEC model to help students identify career paths that align with their interests and preferences. It is important to note that it does not measure intelligence or actual ability.
